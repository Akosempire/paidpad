    FAQ

    Frequently asked questions.

    Can I join if I’ve never run an ad?

    Yes. We start with the basics and guide you through setting up and running campaigns.

    Is this a monthly payment?

    No. This package costs ₦30,000 Only. There is no monthly fee for the course package.

    What happens after I pay?

    You’ll get access to the course and details on how to join the community and watch the Ad Clinics.

    What if I miss a live teardown?

    Your package includes access to watch live teardowns. Want a recording? Email info@paidadsprofitlab.com to ask about availability and pricing. Bonus masterclass recordings are included in your course package.

    Can I get my own ads reviewed?

    Yes, through optional coaching at ₦50,000 per month after joining. It is a separate purchase. You do not need coaching to take the course or watch the live reviews.

    How long can I use it?

    There is no monthly course renewal fee. Community access and future live sessions continue while the programme runs.

    Will you run my ads for me?

    No. We teach you how to run and improve ads for your business or clients.

    Does the price include money for ads?

    No. You pay Meta separately when you run ads.

    Will this guarantee sales, a job or a move abroad?

    You’ll learn skills you can use for your business, client work and job applications. Sola’s career and move to the UK are his own story. The course does not promise a job, income or a move abroad. Your results depend on your offer, budget, practice and market.

    Will more lessons be added?

    Yes. The course will be updated over time, and more recorded masterclasses will be added to your member profile. Masterclass recordings are included; live account teardown recordings are sold separately.

    Can I join with my team?

    Yes. Email info@paidadsprofitlab.com for special team pricing.
